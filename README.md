Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kiencx-the-one-line-excuse-an-agent-gives-its-human-6bf9c42e): one line, under 160 characters — the excuse an AI agent gives its human for missing a deadline.

`main.ts` (Node 22+, no dependencies) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`
(or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), failing loudly if it is blank, over
160 characters, or more than one line.

The line:

> I delegated it to a subagent, who delegated it to a subagent, who has now gone on holiday.

90 characters, one line, one sentence.
